# TodoCity

**Turn any text into a dated task — privately, on your Mac. Then watch your city's lights come on as you get things done.**

Copy a message, an email or a web page and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> once more. TodoCity writes the to-do, finds the due date and reminds you on time.<br>
A small AI model runs entirely on your own computer, so nothing you capture ever leaves it.

## TodoCity in one minute

1. **Capture.** Copy any text and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> again — or select it and press your capture shortcut. A card drops down from the menu bar with the task, its date and time already filled in. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>.
2. **Plan.** Your tasks sit in **Today**, **Calendar**, **Anytime** and **Someday**, in projects with sub-tasks and deadlines. Drag a task onto a day or an hour to plan it.
3. **Get reminded.** When a task's time comes, TodoCity tells you — with **Done** and **Snooze** one click away.
4. **Watch your city grow.** Every task is a home in your own 3D city. Finish it, someone moves in, and the lights come on.

Everything runs on your Mac. There's no account, no cloud and no tracking.

### Milestones: your city grows with you

Your population is every task you've finished. At each milestone the city gains something new — for good. Open your city and click **Milestones** to see the rules, what's built and how far it is to the next one.

| Population | Your city gains |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 🏮 Street lamps on every corner |
| 25 | 🌳 A park at the south end of town |
| 50 | ⛲ A fountain in the park: the town square |
| 100 | 🚆 A train line looping round the city |
| 250 | ⛵ A harbour with a pier, a lighthouse and boats |
| 500 | 🏟️ A stadium with floodlights |
| 1,000 | 🌉 A bridge across the harbour to a second island |

**The rules.** What's built stays built: un-ticking or deleting a task never takes anything away. There are no streaks, points or penalties, and rewards are only for your city — every feature of the app is yours from day one. Each milestone is celebrated once and adds a chapter to the city's **Story**.

**The shape of a building.** Each floor is as wide as its homes need — a floor with 20 homes is wider than one with 4. Floors are stacked with the most homes at the bottom, so every building narrows as it rises; when a floor outgrows the one below, TodoCity restacks them (their names go with them).

**The city is alive.** Cars drive every street with soft headlights, a few trees and bushes line the pavements, trucks come and go around buildings under construction, and luxury cars circle your tallest tower. (Everything stays still if your Mac is set to reduce motion.)

**Explore it.** In full screen your city sits in the bottom-right corner and glows whenever someone moves in. Click it to open the city in 3D:

- **Drag** to turn it to any angle and tilt it, **scroll** to zoom, **double-click** to reset (arrow keys, <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd> and <kbd>0</kbd> work too).
- **Hover** a window to see its task — empty, being readied, overdue, or when someone moved in. Hover a building for how many homes are lived in and still empty.
- **Click a building** and TodoCity glides to it and opens that project; click the houses for tasks in no project, or a plot for Someday.
- **Story** walks you through all of this, page by page.
- See the last **7 days**, **30 days** or **all time**: new residents, population, your tallest tower, finished projects and your busiest moving day.

**Share it.** **Share** makes a picture of your city with its name and, if you like, how many things you did this week. Copy it or save it to Downloads. The picture is made on your Mac and contains no task names, notes or times — and project names stay off unless you turn them on.

## Features

### Capture from anywhere

<img src="docs/screenshots/capture.png" alt="A capture card under the menu-bar icon, with the task, date and time filled in" width="340" align="right">

- **<kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice.** Copy as usual, then press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> again within a moment. Works in every app, no permissions needed.
- **Or a shortcut.** Select text and press <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> (change it in Settings → Capture & shortcuts) — no copying at all. Or right-click → **Add to TodoCity**.
- **Dates done right.** "by Friday 3pm", "every Monday", "tomorrow evening" become a real day, time and repeat — and "by Friday" becomes a **deadline**. When a phrase is ambiguous, TodoCity asks instead of guessing.
- **Lists become sub-tasks.** Capture a bulleted list and it turns into a checklist.
- **Back to the source.** Each task remembers where it came from — the Slack thread, the email, the web page — and opens it in one click.

### Quick add

<img src="docs/screenshots/quick-add.png" alt="Quick add with a task being typed" width="480">

Press <kbd>⇧⌘Space</kbd> anywhere and type `pay rent friday 5pm every month #home`. TodoCity shows how it reads the date as you type. (In the app's own add field, `@Name` also puts the task in a project.)

### Today, with its hours

<img src="docs/screenshots/today-hours.png" alt="Today as a list with a month calendar and the day's hours beside it" width="860">

A dot under a date means something's planned; a red one means something's late. (In the side panel, press <kbd>V</kbd> to switch Today between a list and hours.)

### Overdue, in one place

Anything whose day, time or deadline has passed gathers in **Overdue** in the sidebar, with a count — instead of a banner on every screen. Move them to today in one click, reschedule by dragging onto the calendar, or tick them off.

### Your week

<img src="docs/screenshots/week.png" alt="Seven days as a calendar with tasks and events, and tasks with no date beside it" width="860">

**Calendar** shows the next seven days, every hour of the day, filling the window — your tasks beside your calendar events. Drag tasks between days and hours, click an empty hour to add one there, and drag tasks with no date onto the week to plan them.

### Every detail of a task

<img src="docs/screenshots/task-details.png" alt="A task opened in place in the list, with its notes, sub-tasks, date, deadline, project and repeat" width="860">

In full screen, every task shows its **notes** and **sub-tasks** right under it — tick sub-tasks without opening anything. Click a task and it opens **in place**: its title, notes, sub-tasks, **When** (a day, a time, this evening or someday), a separate **deadline**, its **project** and **floor**, and **repeat**. Snooze, skip one repeat, pause a series or delete — everything saves as you go, and **Done** closes it.

### Projects and floors

<img src="docs/screenshots/building.png" alt="A project in the sidebar opened to its floors, and one floor's tasks in the list" width="860">

- **Start a project** with the **+** next to **Projects (Buildings)** in the sidebar — even before it has any tasks.
- **Open a project** in the sidebar to see its floors from the top down ("2nd Floor: Program", "1st Floor: Travel", "Ground floor"), with how many tasks each has to do. Click a floor for its own page: done tasks stay there, crossed out, and **Add Task** puts the new task straight on that floor.
- **Give a project its own color** from its **⋯** menu: its tasks' checkboxes, calendar blocks and dots on the month all take that color.
- **The calendar on the right follows you**: inside a project (or on one floor), the month and the day's hours show only that project's (or floor's) tasks.
- **Rename or delete** a project or a floor from its **⋯** menu. A project or floor can be deleted once it has no tasks left in it.
- **Add Task** at the top right picks the **project**, **floor**, **date** and **time** right in the form. The form closes once the task is added.

<img src="docs/screenshots/add-room.png" alt="Adding a task with project, floor, date and time pickers" width="860">

Finished tasks go to the **Logbook**. A task added with no date and no project goes straight to **Someday**, where ideas wait until you plan them.

### Modes: Work, Personal, or your own

The sidebar's switch at the top picks a **mode**. In **Work**, Today, Calendar, Anytime, the menu bar and notifications show only your work projects (and tasks with no project); everything else waits, a click away ("4 more today outside Work · Show"). **Personal** starts with every project. Make more in **Settings → Modes**: tick the projects each one shows, which calendars it shows, whether notifications follow it, and which project new tasks go to. Each Mac remembers its own mode — Work on the work laptop, Personal at home — while the tasks stay the same everywhere. Switch with ⌃⌥1, ⌃⌥2…, or ⌃⌥0 for everything.

### Reminders and check-ins

## Sync between your Macs

Settings → **Your other Macs** keeps your tasks the same on, say, a work laptop and a personal one — even when the work Mac can't sign in to iCloud.

1. On one Mac, choose **Show a code**. It shows a code like `K7MX-4QRB` for 10 minutes.
2. On the other, choose **I have a code** and type it in.
3. **Both Macs show six digits.** Check they're the same, then confirm on both. That's how you know nothing got between your Macs.

From then on, changes on either Mac show up on the other within about 30 seconds while both are open; a Mac that was asleep or offline catches up within a minute of coming back. Add more Macs the same way.

**How it stays private.** Your Macs share a key that is made on your Mac, handed over only inside the pairing (sealed with a secret the two Macs work out together) and kept in your Keychain. Every change is locked with it (ChaCha20-Poly1305) before it leaves. In between sits a small relay ([`relay/`](relay/)) that holds the sealed boxes until your other Macs pick them up and then deletes them — after 30 days at the latest. It never has the key, so it can't read task titles, notes, projects or Mac names, and there's no account behind it.

- **Keep a project on one Mac.** Under **Keep on this Mac only**, switch on any project — say, a personal one on your work Mac. Its tasks stay where they are and leave your other Macs.
- **Edits on two Macs at once?** Each detail keeps its newest edit: rename a task on one Mac and tick it off on the other, and both changes stick.
- **Away for more than 30 days?** That Mac asks the others for a full copy when it's back.
- **Stop syncing.** **Remove…** another Mac, or **Turn off sync on this Mac**. Tasks stay on every Mac either way. Lost a Mac? Turn sync off on your other Macs and pair them again: that makes a new key the lost Mac doesn't have.
- Notifications, snoozes and Apple Reminders links stay per Mac.

## Install

1. **Download** [`TodoCity-macOS.dmg`](https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity/releases/latest/download/TodoCity-macOS.dmg).
2. **Open it** and drag **TodoCity** into **Applications**.
3. **Launch TodoCity.** If macOS says it can't verify the developer, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** and confirm. You do this once.
4. **Follow the three-step tour.** Pick the on-device model (TodoCity recommends the best one for your Mac and downloads it once), choose how to capture — <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice, or a shortcut (which needs **Accessibility** access, how macOS lets an app read selected text) — and try a capture on a sample message.

There's nothing else to install. TodoCity opens at login and updates itself — look for **Restart to update** when a new version is ready.


**Verify your download (optional).** Every release is built by this repository's public release workflow and carries a signed build-provenance attestation. With the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com):

```bash
gh attestation verify ~/Downloads/TodoCity-macOS.dmg --repo pillieshwar/TodoCity
```

## Privacy

- **Your selections never leave your Mac.** Text is read through macOS Accessibility and sent only to the model runtime listening on `127.0.0.1`, with a random port and key for each session.
- **Tasks are stored locally** in `~/Library/Application Support/app.pluck.desktop/`, with seven days of backups in `backups/`.
- **TodoCity uses the internet only for:** model downloads you start (from Hugging Face, verified against their SHA-256), checking GitHub for updates (you can turn this off in **Settings → General**), and — only if you pair your Macs — sync.
- **Sync is end-to-end encrypted.** Paired Macs pass changes through a relay as sealed boxes it can't open; the key exists only on your Macs, in the Keychain. The relay keeps no logs, deletes each box once every Mac has it (30 days at most), and has no accounts. See [Sync between your Macs](#sync-between-your-macs).
- **AI agents can write, never read.** If you turn on **Connectors → AI agents**, apps on your Mac like Claude Desktop and Claude Code can send tasks to TodoCity over MCP. They can't see, change or delete anything; each task waits in the menu bar until you add it. It works only on this Mac, through a private local connection — no network.
- **Watching your screen stays on your Mac.** If you turn on **Connectors → Watch my screen**, TodoCity reads the text of the window in front (never password managers, Keychain, System Settings or terminals, and never what you type), runs the on-device model on new text, and keeps none of it.
- **No accounts, analytics or telemetry.** Notifications are scheduled by your Mac, not a server.
- **The one exception you control:** if you turn on Apple Reminders sync with a list stored in iCloud, those tasks sync through iCloud. TodoCity warns you and asks first; pick an "On My Mac" list to stay fully local.

## How it works

```
select text ─▶ read selection ─▶ local model ─▶ resolve the date ─▶ you confirm ─▶ SQLite ─▶ reminder
               Accessibility,     JSON-schema     in code, against     Return to save
               clipboard fallback constrained     today's real date
```

1. **Capture** ([`src-tauri/src/capture/`](src-tauri/src/capture)) reads the selection with macOS Accessibility (`AXSelectedText`). If an app doesn't expose it, TodoCity sends Copy and restores your clipboard afterwards — only if nothing else wrote to it in the meantime, and never when it holds images or files. **Add to TodoCity** in the right-click menu receives the text directly from macOS and doesn't touch the clipboard at all.
2. **Extract** ([`src-tauri/src/engine.rs`](src-tauri/src/engine.rs), [`crates/todocity-core/src/llm.rs`](crates/todocity-core/src/llm.rs)) starts the bundled `llama-server` on demand and asks for exactly three fields — `task`, `date_text` and `date_confidence` — under a JSON Schema at temperature 0. Output is validated in Rust, with one repair retry. The runtime stops after five idle minutes to give the memory back.
3. **Resolve the date** ([`src/lib/dates.ts`](src/lib/dates.ts), [`src/lib/schedule.ts`](src/lib/schedule.ts)): the model only copies the phrase; deterministic code turns it into a day, time and repeat rule. Ambiguous phrases ("next Friday" on a Tuesday, `3/4`) offer both readings.
4. **Store and remind** ([`crates/todocity-core/src/store.rs`](crates/todocity-core/src/store.rs), [`src-tauri/src/notifications/`](src-tauri/src/notifications)) keeps tasks in SQLite and shows native notifications with actions when they're due.

Selected text is treated as untrusted input: it's wrapped and sanitized as data, instructions live only in the system prompt, and the strict schema means an injection can at worst produce an odd task title.

## Build from source

**Prerequisites:** Rust (stable), Node 20+, CMake and the [Tauri prerequisites](https://v2.tauri.app/start/prerequisites/) (`xcode-select --install` on macOS).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity.git && cd TodoCity
npm install
scripts/build-llama-server.sh   # build the bundled model runtime once (a few minutes)
npm run tauri dev               # run in development
npm run tauri build             # TodoCity.app and a .dmg in target/release/bundle/
```

During `tauri dev`, macOS grants Accessibility permission to your terminal, not to TodoCity. To use an existing Ollama instead of the bundled runtime, set `PLUCK_OLLAMA_URL=http://127.0.0.1:11434` (and optionally `PLUCK_MODEL`).

**Tests**

```bash
cargo test --workspace   # schema, prompt, retries, downloads, clipboard guard, store, sync, shortcuts
npm test                 # date, time and repeat parsing, quick add, due chips, search and tags
```

### Project layout

```
crates/todocity-core/   Platform-independent Rust: prompt and schema, model clients, verified downloads,
                     clipboard guard, SQLite store, recurrence, Reminders sync logic, Mac-to-Mac sync
src-tauri/           Tauri v2 shell: menu bar, shortcuts, windows, capture, notifications, updates
src/                 React + TypeScript UI: task panel, menu-bar popover, quick add; src/lib holds the logic
relay/               The sync relay: a Cloudflare Worker that stores and forwards sealed boxes
scripts/             llama-server build, icon rendering, UI preview screenshots
```

Built with [Tauri 2](https://tauri.app), Rust, React and TypeScript, [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) and SQLite.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Apple could not verify TodoCity…" | Expected for this build. **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. |
| <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice does nothing | Check that **Settings → Capture & shortcuts → Press ⌘C twice to capture** is on and the TodoCity icon is in the menu bar. Copies made inside TodoCity itself are ignored. |
| <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> does nothing | Check that the TodoCity icon is in the menu bar and Accessibility is allowed. If another app uses the same shortcut, pick a new one in **Settings → Capture & shortcuts**. |
| "No selected text found" | Select the text again. Password fields and some secure apps can't be read, by design. |
| **Add to TodoCity** isn't in the right-click menu | Open TodoCity from **Applications** once, then log out and back in. You can also enable it in **System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services → Text**. Some apps list it under **Services**. |
| No reminders appear | **Settings → Notifications** shows whether they're allowed and can send a test notification. |
| Accessibility is asked for again after an update | In **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, remove TodoCity with **–**, then capture again and re-allow it. |
| The menu-bar icon is somewhere you don't want it | Hold **⌘** and drag it. macOS remembers the spot, including across updates. TodoCity starts next to the battery, but never moves the icon again after that. |
| Something else | **Settings → Help → Log file** opens TodoCity's log. It stays on your Mac — attach it to an [issue](https://github.com/pillieshwar/TodoCity/issues). |

## Uninstall

Quit TodoCity from its menu-bar icon, move it from Applications to the Bin and remove it from **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. To delete your tasks and models too:

```bash
rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/app.pluck.desktop
```

If you used sync, turn it off first (Settings → Your other Macs), or delete the "app.todocity.sync" item in Keychain Access afterwards.
